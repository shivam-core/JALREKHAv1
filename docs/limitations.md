# Limitations

- **Prototype Nature:** This is a what-if planning simulator. It does not provide real-time forecasting, operational rescue instructions, or officially sanctioned shelter status.
- **Hydrology (HAND):** The water level is a relative HAND scenario parameter, not a calibrated hydrological forecast linked to river discharge or precipitation.
- **Origins & Population:** The model assumes people are already at the origins (assembly points). It does not model the journey from individual homes.
- **Demographics:** The optimization treats individuals as uniform units, not preserving family groups.
- **Vehicles:** Vehicle capacity, congestion, and traffic are not explicitly modeled. 
- **Infrastructure:** Bridges and tunnels are subject to DEM resolution limits. Road closures must be actively modeled by the planner.
