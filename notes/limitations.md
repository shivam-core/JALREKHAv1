# Limitations
- Copernicus DEM is a surface model: buildings and trees raise heights.
- HAND is a "bathtub" index: no flow dynamics, no drain capacity, one nearest drainage line.
- Shelter capacities are assumptions (school 300, college 500, university 800, hospital 100,
community centre 200).
- Population is a proxy (25 people per road node in a cell), not census data.
- The discharge-to-level mapping is a heuristic, not a rating curve.
- Road speeds are OSM defaults; real traffic in a flood is slower.
